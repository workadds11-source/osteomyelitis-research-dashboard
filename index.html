Research question & objectives

All objectives preserved from the study documents

Research question

What are the clinical patterns and treatment outcomes of osteomyelitis among patients attending CHUK and RMTH between 2023 and 2025?

General objective

To determine the clinical patterns and treatment outcomes of osteomyelitis among patients attending CHUK and RMTH between 2023 and 2025.

Specific objective 1

Describe socio-demographic characteristics: sex, age and educational level.

Specific objective 2

Identify clinical patterns: acute vs chronic, site of infection and staging classification.

Specific objective 3

Evaluate treatment approaches: antibiotics, surgery and combined therapy.

Specific objective 4

Assess outcomes: cure rate, recurrence, complications and duration of hospital stay.
